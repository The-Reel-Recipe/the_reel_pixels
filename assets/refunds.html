This is part of the S37 Terms of Use. It explains what you pay for, when you get it, and when money or paint comes back. Please read section 6 before you buy paint.

Arabic version: /assets/refunds.ar.html. The Arabic version is the one that applies.

1. Who you are dealing with

S37 is run by Mohab Yasser Albahrawy, an individual, of Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, Cairo Governorate, Egypt. ·

Write to us at mohabhelp@gmail.com. For anything about money, mohabhelp@gmail.com.

When you pay, your money goes to the InstaPay handle shown on your payment screen. That handle belongs to the person named above. It is not a company account and it is not an escrow account.

We keep a record of every order, transfer, decision and refund for 5 years, because Egyptian tax and commercial law requires it. That is also why we cannot delete a payment record when you ask us to erase your data — see the Privacy Notice.

S37 is a small operation. Payments are checked by hand, by a person, one at a time. That shapes everything below.

2. The only two things you can pay for

Paint. Prepaid pixels. You buy an amount — for example 25, 100 or 500 pixels — and it sits on your account until you use it. Larger amounts cost less per pixel. The price per pixel and the price of each pack are shown in the app before you order.

Brand booking. A business pre-books space on next month's wall for its logo, at the per-pixel price shown in the app. Bookings are for approved brand accounts only.

Everything else on S37 is free. You get a batch of free pixels — currently 50 — and when you have spent the whole batch a fresh one arrives an hour later. You never need to pay to paint.

To buy paint you need an account with an email and a password. Paint lives on the account, not on the browser, so a guest cannot buy it.

3. What paint is

Paint is permission to use a feature of S37. It is not money.

  • Not cash, not a wallet, not a deposit, not electronic money.
  • Not a voucher for goods; there are no goods.
  • Not an investment; it earns nothing.
  • You cannot send it to another person, sell it, or cash it out.

It does one thing: it lets you paint pixels on the S37 wall.

Paint is counted in pixels, not in pounds. If we change the price of paint later, the number of pixels on your account does not change.

4. How payment works

We take InstaPay bank transfers only. No cards, no payment gateway, nothing charged to you automatically, ever.

We will never ask you for your bank password, your PIN, your OTP or your card number. If anyone claiming to be S37 asks for those, it is not us.

To pay:

  1. Order in the app. You get a code that looks like S37-XXXX.
  2. Send the transfer from your own bank or wallet app to the handle shown.
  3. Put the S37-XXXX code in the transfer note. This is how we find your money.
  4. Come back to the app and give us the transfer reference and the handle you paid from.

A screenshot of your transfer is optional. It helps us find your money faster. It is seen by the people who check payments, so send only the transfer you are asking about — and read what the Privacy Notice says about what a banking screenshot shows before you decide.

Once you press send in your bank app, the money leaves you and arrives with us. That happens between banks. S37 is not part of it and cannot see it, stop it or reverse it.

5. Delivery — when you get what you paid for

Paint. A person checks every transfer against our own bank record. Paint is added to your account only after that person confirms the money arrived. This is usually done within the hour, and often much faster. It is not a guarantee — one person, a phone, and sometimes a night's sleep.

Brand space. Your logo goes up when the wall resets on the 1st, and holds its space for that month. Payment is confirmed first; the artwork is reviewed after.

Your order screen shows a deadline. That deadline is the promise.

  • If we confirm the money before the deadline, your paint is credited or your space is held for review.
  • If the deadline passes and you have not told us a transfer reference, the order closes. For a brand booking the pixels go back on the wall for someone else. Nothing is owed either way, because nothing was paid and nothing was delivered.
  • If you have told us a reference, the order does not close on you. It stays open until a person has checked. If we cannot find your transfer we tell you so, and section 10 applies.

Nothing is credited, and no space goes live, until a person confirms the money.

6. Refunds on paint

Paint is not refundable in money. Not the part you have spent, and not the part you have not.

We say that plainly because it is a real term and you should decide with it in front of you, not discover it afterwards. Here is the reasoning, so you can judge it:

  • Paint is delivered the moment we confirm your transfer, and you can spend it a second later. When you order, you ask us to credit it as soon as we confirm the money, so you can paint straight away; you tick that on the payment screen and we record that you did.
  • Once it is on your account it cannot be un-delivered. What you bought was the ability to paint, and you have it.
  • If the work you paint is turned down, you have not lost the paint — it comes straight back to your balance (section 7).
  • Paint does not expire while S37 runs, so unspent paint is not lost value. It is waiting.

So, to be completely clear, there is no cash back:

  • because you changed your mind;
  • for paint you have spent;
  • for paint you have not spent;
  • because the wall was reset on the 1st;
  • because your drawing was turned down — your paint comes back instead (section 7).

We do not do partial cash refunds of a paint balance, and paint cannot be transferred to another account.

Check the pack and the price on screen before you send the transfer. The screen shows exactly how much paint you are buying and exactly what it costs.

6.1 When we do return money anyway

This section is a rule about paint that has been delivered. It is not a way of keeping money for something you never got. We return money, in full and without deduction, in all of these cases:

  1. We received money and never delivered anything against it — your order closed, expired, or was marked "not received", and the transfer turns out to have arrived. Tell us and we put it right (section 10).
  2. The payment came from someone without legal capacity to make it, or from an account they had no permission to use (section 16).
  3. We close S37 — the price you paid for unspent paint comes back (section 9).
  4. We end your access for our own reasons, or for anything short of fraud on your side — the price you paid for unspent paint comes back (Terms, section 21).
  5. Egyptian law gives you a right to your money that we cannot exclude. Then the law wins, we honour it, and we do not make you argue for it. See section 15.

7. What does come back: paint, not money

S37 checks every batch of pixels by hand before it goes on the wall. If your work does not go up, the paint goes back on your account so you can use it again. The app puts it like this:

"If a batch is turned down, the paint comes straight back."
«لو دفعة اترفضت، البوية بترجعلك فورًا.»

That happens automatically in four cases:

  1. A moderator rejects your batch.
  2. Your batch is still waiting when its time runs out and expires.
  3. Approved pixels of yours are later taken down (section 12.6).
  4. Your batch is still waiting for a decision when the wall resets on the 1st.

In every one of those the paint you spent on that batch returns to your balance and you can spend it again immediately.

Free pixels do not come back. If a batch used free pixels and was turned down, those free pixels stay spent. A refusal is a decision about your drawing, not extra free pixels. Note how the free batch works: the hour's wait only starts once you have spent the whole batch, so if you had some left they are still there, and if you had none the next batch is already on its way.

Paint returning to your balance is a feature of the service, not a refund of your purchase. It does not become cash at any point.

8. The monthly reset

The whole wall is wiped at 00:00 Cairo time on the 1st of every month. Every pixel disappears and everyone starts on a white wall.

  • Pixels you painted are gone. There is no refund for painted pixels that were wiped. The wipe is the product and it is described throughout the app.
  • Your unspent paint balance is not touched by the wipe. Paint does not expire on the 1st, or on any other date, while S37 runs.
  • Buying paint late in the month gives you fewer days of wall. Worth thinking about before you buy 500 pixels on the 30th.

Wiping the wall clears the picture. It does not erase our records of your orders and payments — see section 1 and the Privacy Notice.

9. If S37 stops running

If we decide to close S37 we will give at least 60 days notice in the app, and by any other means we have of reaching you. During that period:

  • the wall stays open and paintable, so you can spend the paint you already have;
  • we stop selling paint and stop taking bookings from the day we announce it;
  • we refund, in money, the price you paid for any paint still unspent when the wall closes, to the InstaPay handle you paid from, within 30 days of closing. You do not need to ask;
  • we refund in full any brand booking we took money for and did not deliver.

If S37 is closed suddenly by something outside our control, the last two still apply and we do them as soon as we are able.

10. If we disagree about whether the money arrived

Understand this first: there is no chargeback and no dispute button. We cannot reverse an InstaPay transfer, and neither can the app. Only your bank can act on a transfer you sent, and only in limited cases. Nothing here changes what your bank will or will not do.

So we handle it by hand, and we take it seriously.

If you sent money and your order closed, was marked "not received", or expired — tell us. Our system does not record a debt on its own in that situation. If you do not tell us, nothing happens.

Send to mohabhelp@gmail.com, or through the app:

  • your order code (S37-XXXX),
  • the transfer reference from your receipt,
  • the handle or number you paid from,
  • the date, time and amount,
  • a screenshot of the transfer if you have one.

We check our own bank record against what you send. Then one of three things happens:

  • We find your money. We reopen the order and deliver what you bought — the paint, or the booked space if it is still available.
  • We find your money but cannot deliver — for example the space was taken in the meantime. We refund you in full (section 13).
  • We cannot find it. We tell you exactly what we searched and over what dates. If the money never reached our account we cannot credit it and cannot refund it, because we do not have it. At that point the question is one for your bank.

What the reference proves. The reference and handle you give us are what we search for in our own bank record. We cannot check them against your bank — nobody can, from outside it. If we find no matching transfer, the reference on its own does not establish that one was made. That is why we tell you what we searched, so you can take it further.

11. Wrong amount, duplicate, or mistaken transfers

  • You paid less than the order. We do not credit a part-order. Tell us and either send the difference before the order deadline, or we refund what arrived.
  • You paid more than the order. We credit the order and the extra becomes paint on your account, rounded down to whole pixels. If you would rather have the extra back in money, say so and we send it back.
  • You paid twice for the same order. We refund the duplicate, or credit it against a new order — your choice.
  • You forgot the S37-XXXX code. We will still try to find your transfer from the reference, the handle you paid from, the amount and the time. Give us all four. It takes longer and it is not always possible.
  • You sent money to the wrong handle. If it never reached our account we cannot help beyond confirming that. Contact your bank.

Refunds only ever go back to the handle you paid from. We will not send a refund to a different handle, name or account, whoever asks. This protects you.

12. Brand bookings

12.1 What you are buying

Space on next month's wall for your logo, at the per-pixel price shown when you order. Your logo goes up when the wall resets on the 1st and holds that space for the month, until the next reset. The smallest and largest logo the app will build are shown when you place it.

12.2 Price and quantity

You pay for the pixels actually reserved for you. If part of the area you chose was already taken, those pixels are dropped and you are not charged for them, so the count and the total on the confirmation screen may be lower than what you drew — never higher.

Your price is frozen when you place the order. If we change the per-pixel price afterwards, your open order is unaffected.

12.3 The hold

Placing an order reserves your pixels while your transfer comes through. The deadline is on your order screen. If we have not heard from you by then, the order closes and the pixels go back for someone else to book. Nothing is owed either way, because nothing was paid and nothing was delivered. If you have told us a reference, section 5 applies instead — the order stays open until a person has checked.

12.4 Paying is not approval

Your logo is checked by a person, exactly like every other pixel. A confirmed payment does not mean your logo is approved. We check the payment first and look at the artwork after.

If we cannot give you the space you paid for — for any reason — we owe you your money back in full. That includes a logo never decided before its month went up, and a month that never happened.

12.5 If your logo is turned down

The app promises this at the moment of purchase, and we mean it:

"Your logo is reviewed like every other pixel. If it is turned down, the payment is refunded to the InstaPay handle you paid from."
«اللوجو بيتراجع زي أي بكسل. لو اترفض، الفلوس بترجع لحساب إنستاباي اللي دفعت منه.»

We refund the booking in full when we turn a logo down, including when we turn it down because it broke our content rules. We would rather give the money back than argue about it.

What we do not do is give you an open-ended right to keep resubmitting at our cost. If your submissions repeatedly break the rules we may refuse to take further bookings from you, and we may refuse a booking whose link points somewhere we are not willing to send our visitors.

12.6 Taking a logo down after it is live

We may take down live pixels — including paid, approved ones — if we later find they break our rules, infringe someone's rights, are unlawful, or if we are required to. We do not do this lightly.

If we take down a paid brand booking, we refund the booking in full. We do not reduce the refund for the days it was already on the wall. This applies whatever the reason for the takedown, including a takedown caused by your own link or artwork.

12.7 What a booking does not buy

We do not guarantee position, visibility, traffic, clicks or results, and a booking is not exclusive. Our responsibility for anything to do with a booking is limited as set out in section 22 of the Terms of Use, which is the only limit that applies.

13. How we pay refunds

  • By InstaPay, to the handle you paid from, and to no other handle.
  • Within 7 of agreeing that a refund is due.
  • We deduct no fee. You get back what we received. If your own bank charges you to receive a transfer, that charge is between you and your bank.
  • We record the outbound transfer reference against your order, and you can ask us for it.
  • You will see the refund marked in MY PIXELS once we have sent it. If you do not see the money in your account after that, tell us — a transfer can fail at our end and we will send it again.

14. Complaints

Write to mohabhelp@gmail.com, or use the contact option in the app. Include your order code (S37-XXXX), the transfer reference, the handle you paid from, the amount, the date, and what went wrong.

We read every message and we always answer. We aim to answer within 7 days. If something will take longer, we say so and tell you when to expect an answer.

If we cannot resolve it between us, you can take your complaint to the Consumer Protection Agency (hotline 19588, cpa.gov.eg, or info@cpa.gov.eg) or to the courts. Nothing here requires you to come to us first.

15. Your rights under Egyptian law

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any right you have under Egyptian law that cannot be removed or limited by agreement, including under the Consumer Protection Law (Law 181 of 2018) and its executive regulations.

If a court or the competent authority decides that any part of this policy cannot apply to you, that part is treated as removed and the rest still applies. Where a limit we have written is wider than the law allows, it applies at the widest the law does allow.

Where the law gives you a right stronger than what this policy offers, the law wins, and we will honour it without making you argue for it.

16. Capacity, and payments made without permission

You must have full legal capacity under Egyptian law to place an order. You must also be paying from an account that is your own, or one you have the account holder's permission to use.

If a payment was made by someone without capacity to make it, or from an account they had no permission to use, we return the money in full to the account it came from, without charge and without deduction, as soon as we can establish the facts. We cancel the order and remove any pixels painted with it. Section 6 does not apply to this.

Tell us at mohabhelp@gmail.com with the order code or the transfer details. We do not ask you to prove more than enough to identify the payment.

17. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. The version in force is the one published when you place your order — a change does not apply backwards to an order you have already placed and paid for.

If we make a change that materially affects money you have already paid us, we say so in the app and, where we have an email address for you, by email. We will not apply such a change to paint you have already bought unless you accept it; if you do not, you keep the old terms, you can spend the paint, and you can ask us to refund the unspent part at the price you paid.

18. Language, law and courts

This policy is published in Arabic and in English. If the two versions differ, the Arabic version applies.

It is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt. Disputes go to the courts of Cairo, without affecting any right you have to complain to a consumer protection body or, as a consumer, to bring a claim where you live.
