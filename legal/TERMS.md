## 6. Your identity on S37

**Guest.** The first time you open S37 we create an anonymous identity for you and store it in a cookie in your browser. It is how we know which pixels are yours. Details, including how long it lasts, are in the Privacy Notice.

**This matters:** a guest identity lives in one browser. Clear your browsing data, switch phone, or use private browsing, and that identity is gone — and so is anything on it. We cannot get it back for you. That is why paint can only be bought on an account.

**Painter account.** You can add an email address and a password to your identity. Everything you have already painted stays with you and you can sign in on any device.

**Brand account.** Businesses apply with their business details. We review every application by hand, and we may approve or refuse it.

**For all accounts:** one email address, one account; give us true details; keep your password to yourself, because anything done from your account is treated as done by you; and do not create extra identities to get around the limits in section 9.

## 7. Free pixels

Free pixels are a feature we offer, not something you own or buy.

- The size of the batch and the wait are shown in the app. We can change them, or stop offering free pixels, at any time.
- **Free pixels are not returned if your work is refused, or if it is still waiting when the month ends.** A refusal is a decision about that drawing, not extra allowance. Paid paint is returned — see section 12.
- Free pixels have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred or refunded.

## 8. The wall is erased every month

**At 00:00 Cairo time on the 1st of every month, every pixel on the wall is erased** — yours, everyone's, free and paid alike. Nothing carries over.

The reset date is shown in the app before you paint and before you pay. Pixels you paint on the 31st live for less than a day; that is your choice to make with the date in front of you.

The reset runs on a timer on our server. It may run a few minutes late, or later if the service is down.

**Your unspent paint balance is not touched by the reset** (section 16).

**What is erased, and what is not.** The public wall is erased. Our records are not. Before the wall is cleared we save a picture of it, and we keep that picture — it is a flat image of colours with no names or account details in it. We also keep the record of what you submitted, our moderation records, and our payment records. How long, and why, is in the Privacy Notice.

So "wiped clean every month" is true of the wall you look at. It is not a promise that we delete our records.

**Refunds** go by InstaPay to **the handle you paid from**, and to no other handle, name or account, whoever asks. That protects you. Timing and fees are in the Refund Policy.
